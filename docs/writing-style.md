# Writing style

Use a single accessible paragraph balancing scientific contributions and the
person's documented life or career. Explain concretely why the work matters to
physics. Identify the actual discipline of adjacent scientists and mathematicians.

The existing descriptions range from 111 to 211 words. Aim for roughly 120–175
words, allowing justified variation. This is editorial guidance, not a hard gate.
Use clear, respectful language and only verified dates, affiliations, and claims.
Avoid portraying identity or hardship as the person's main achievement. Do not
introduce facts from memory. Avoid time-sensitive “currently” claims unless verified.

Match the newsletter's readable biographical tone. Picture may remain empty.
Keep source evidence in the research bundle rather than expanding the CSV schema.
Historical claims are not automatically reliable just because they are examples.
