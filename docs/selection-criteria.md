# Selection criteria

Eligible candidates worked substantially in physics, made a direct contribution to
physics, or developed methods foundational to an identifiable area of physics.
For adjacent scientists and mathematicians, document actual substantial influence;
possible applications are insufficient. Formal job title is not a requirement.

Exclude every person already in any CSV beneath data/. Check aliases and ambiguous
name matches. Never infer identity or barriers from names, photos, or nationality.
Select for a documented scientific contribution and a documented life or career
experience relevant to inclusion, persistence, discrimination, or a nontraditional
path. Compare evidence quality and scientific relevance, not presumed identities.
